## Observations for implementers (not typos)

- **Bond timing (p.7, pdf 13):** `B_{h,t}`, `B^{*}_{h,t}` are holdings at the *beginning* of period t. The
  second row of (2) contains the purchases `B_{h,t+1}`, `B^{*}_{h,t+1}`; the fourth row contains the maturing `B_{h,t}`, `B^{*}_{h,t}`.
- **Time index carries meaning in two symbol-table rows:** `g_{z,t}` (time-varying productivity growth) and
  `g_{z}` (long-run parameter) are listed separately, as are `\Delta inv_{t}` (exogenous share) and `\Delta inv`
  (its steady state). This departs from the drop-the-time-index rule so the keys stay unambiguous.
- **(15):** the productivity term is dated `g_{z;t-1,t+k-1}`, while the definition (15.i1) is given for
  `g_{z;t,t+k}`. The shift by one period matches indexation to lagged productivity in (14).
- **3.u1 (domestic premium shock):** printed in **levels** with constant `(1-0.99)`, so its mean is 1.
  That is unlike the log-AR(1) example in footnote 4.
- **(4):** depreciation is written as the product `S_{t+1}/S_{t} · S_{t}/S_{t-1}` (with target ratios).
  It was not simplified.
- **(6):** the shock order is `\varepsilon^{DRP}\varepsilon^{RP}`; (2) and the text use `\varepsilon^{RP}\varepsilon^{DRP}`.
  Kept as printed.
- **(14) vs (15)/(16):** the exponent of `g_{z}` is printed `(1-\chi_{W,g_{z}})` in parentheses in (14),
  and without parentheses in (15) and (16). Kept as printed.
- **Tilde accents:** `\widetilde{I}` (wide tilde) on p. 10 (pdf 16) is printed in a different style from
  `\tilde{W}` (narrow tilde) on pp. 11–12 (pdf 17–18). They denote different objects. In the final edition both are written with `\tilde` (accent width is not preserved for single symbols; see README).
- **`\Xi_{t}` (lump-sum transfers)** sits on the expenditure side of (2) with a plus sign. The lump-sum tax
  `T_{t}` sits on the income side with a minus sign.
- **(12):** the large parenthesis encloses two centred rows. They are rendered as a nested `aligned`
  (one sum).
- **`\varphi^{W}_{t}` (wage markup)** is used in (15)–(16) but defined only later, on p.14 (pdf 20, chunk T02).
- **Tax rates** `\tau^{N}, \tau^{W_{h}}, \tau^{K}, \tau^{D}` carry a time subscript here. p.25 (pdf 31,
  chunk T04) states they are constant. They are classified as parameters in the symbol table.
