# T13 notes: D.10 Observation Equations, D.11 AR(1) processes (PDF pp.128-132, printed 122-126)

Method: every equation was read from the 300-DPI halves, then zoomed at 900-2400 DPI. Glyph identity
(Greek letters, accents, star size and vertical position, signs) was also cross-checked against the
PDF's embedded font glyphs (CM font names/codes via pdfplumber). The images remain the source of truth.
Coverage: (291)-(331), 41 numbered blocks, each once. Prose has two defining inline statements
(tp after (312), tp* after (318)). Appendix D ends on PDF p.132; p.133 starts Appendix E.

No glyph remains uncertain after zooming, so there are no ⚠ UNCERTAIN items.

## Suspected typos / inconsistencies in the paper (all transcribed AS PRINTED)

1. (306) Nominal depreciation rate: prints $\hat{c}_{t}-\hat{c}_{t-1}$ (font glyph is italic c,
   same as consumption in (292)). The nominal-depreciation identity (263) and Appendix B (105) use
   the real exchange rate $\hat{s}_{t}-\hat{s}_{t-1}$, so this is very likely a typo for $\hat{s}$.
   Implementers should check against (263)/(105).
2. Inflation-target term: (297) prints $\hat{\bar{\pi}}_{t}$ (hat over bar, as in (260)), but
   (298), (299), (300 RHS), (304), (305) and (306) print $\bar{\pi}_{t}$ with no hat. The no-hat
   versions probably mean the same deviation $\hat{\bar{\pi}}_{t}$.
3. (300): the LHS has a full-width overline (drawn as a rule in the PDF, unlike the bar accent) plus
   a star: $4\overline{\pi}_{t}^{*}$. The RHS has only domestic terms, $4(\bar{\pi}_{t}+\pi-1)$. The data
   list (p.35) names the observed series "Inflation target (annualized) $(4\cdot\overline{\pi}_{t})$"
   with no star, so the star on the LHS may be a typo. In (318), $\bar{\pi}_{t}^{*}$ is the foreign target.
4. (312) domestic forward real rate: the term inside is $\hat{fw}_{ri^{*},t}$ (narrow hat centred
   over "fw", subscript $ri^{*}$, foreign). It is the same symbol as in the foreign IS curve (286) and
   in (318), which use a wide hat $\widehat{fw}_{ri^{*},t}$. (262) and Appendix B (108) relate the
   domestic forward rate to $\widehat{rr}_{t}^{fw}$ (domestic), so the domestic equation may be meant
   to use $\widehat{rr}^{fw}$ rather than the foreign-subscript term. Transcribed as printed with
   `\hat{fw}` (narrow hat), since only the hat width differs from (318).
5. (320)/(321): D.11 gives AR(1) forms for $EX_{\Delta S}$ and $EX_{\Delta W}$. Appendix B (text after
   (105)/(106)) says both are i.i.d., and Table 3 estimates no AR coefficient for them (only S.D.s).
   The innovation superscripts are $S$ and $W$, while the coefficients carry $\Delta S$ and $\Delta W$.
6. (331): the subscript is printed "$\Delta POil$" (lowercase i, l), but (316) prints $EX_{\Delta POIL,t}$.
   Presumably the same variable.
7. (307) LHS printed $\Delta {Y^{*}}_{t}$ (subscript t after the star). Transcribed as
   `\Delta {Y^{*}}_{t}`; it is the same series as $\Delta Y_{t}^{*}$ in the data list.
8. Two star styles: in the $EX$ subscripts $EX_{\Delta Y*,t}$, $EX_{\Delta WT*,t}$ (307, 319, 329, 330) and
   in $g_{\Delta WT*}$ (330), the asterisk is a subscript-size glyph on the subscript baseline
   (transcribed `Y*`, `WT*`). Elsewhere ($\Delta P_{Y^{*},t}$, $\rho_{EX}^{Y^{*}}$, $g_{\Delta Y^{*}}$,
   $\eta_{EX}^{WT^{*}}$, etc.) it is a raised superscript (transcribed `^{*}`). Same symbols, different
   typesetting. In the sentence after (318) "tp*" is a full-size inline asterisk, while (318) has $tp^{*}$.
9. (297): the subscript of $\Delta P_{Y,t}^{M}$ is printed "Y, t" with the t lowered and spaced; similar
   lowered t in $\Delta P_{C,t}$, $\Delta P_{I,t}$, $\Delta P_{Y^{*},t}$, $\Delta P_{X^{*},t}$. Layout only.
10. (313) prints capital $S_{CA,t}$; the data list (p.35) writes $s_{CA,t}$.
11. (314)/(315) use lowercase $\tau_{c}$, $\hat{\tau}_{t}^{c}$, $\tau_{n}$, $\hat{\tau}_{t}^{n}$. D.6
    eqs (252)/(255) write $\hat{\tau}_{t}^{C}$, $\hat{\tau}_{t}^{N}$ (uppercase). Transcribed as printed.
12. (311)/(310) use lowercase $\hat{p}_{x}$ / $\hat{p}_{x}^{*}$. D.5.5 (249)-(250) write $\hat{p}_{X,t}$.
13. (328) leaves $EX_{\Delta G,t}$ with no AR process of its own (it is determined by this restriction),
    consistent with Appendix B (103). Weights printed: $c/y$, $i/y$, $\Delta inv$, $s_{G}$, $s_{X}$, $im/y$.
14. (301) uses $\chi_{E}$, $\xi_{E}$, $\hat{e}_{t}$, $\hat{\epsilon}_{OB\_E,t}$; section 4.1 eq. (95)
    writes the same equation with EM labels. The shock symbol here is lunate $\epsilon$ (cmmi
    "epsilon"), not $\varepsilon$. The same holds for $\hat{\epsilon}_{OB\_DPY,t}$ (297) and $\hat{\epsilon}_{fwd\_ob,t}$ (312, 318).

## Transcription choices (not paper errors)
- (293): the hat is printed over a dotless i, so it is written `\hat{\imath}_{t}`.
- (296), (319): wide hat over "im" (`\widehat{im}`); in (319) the star is a superscript of the hatted group.
- (307), (319): $\hat{\tilde{z}}$ is a hat over a tilde (confirmed at 2400 DPI and by font glyph positions).
- (317): the wide hat covers "inv" only, written `\Delta \widehat{inv}_{t}`. $\Delta inv$ without a
  subscript is the steady-state share.
- The underscores in labels ($OB\_DPY$, $OB\_E$, $fwd\_ob$, $C\_OB$, $N\_OB$) are printed underscores
  (text-font "_"), written `\_`.
- (312) and (318) trailing commas, and all trailing periods, were dropped.
