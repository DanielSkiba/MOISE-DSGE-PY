## Suspected typos / inconsistencies in the paper (transcribed as printed)
1. **γ_B prior mean (Table 2 vs text).** The 4.4.3 text (PDF p.54) says the prior mean of γ_B was set to the NAWM result **(0.1)**. Table 2 (PDF p.56) prints the prior mean as **0.01** (prior std 0.01; posterior mode 0.012). One of the two is a typo; the table value is consistent with the posterior. The CSV keeps 0.01 as printed.
2. **Markup AR-coefficient labels differ across the paper.**
   - Table 2 prints $\rho_{\varphi^{H}}$, $\rho_{\varphi^{*}}$, $\rho_{\varphi^{W}}$, $\rho_{\varphi^{X}}$.
   - The 4.4.2 list, Table 5 and Figure 3 (p.62) print $\rho_{H}$, $\rho^{*}$, $\rho_{W}$, $\rho_{X}$; eq. (20) uses $\rho_{W}$.
   - Table 3 S.D. labels are $\eta^{\varphi^{H}}$ etc., while 4.4.2 lists the innovations as $\eta_{t}^{H}$, $\eta_{t}^{*}$, $\eta_{t}^{W}$, $\eta_{t}^{X}$; eq. (20) uses $\eta_{t}^{W}$.

   The values are identical (Table 2 modes = Table 5 baseline), so these are the same parameters.
3. **Inventory shock: case of INV.** The 4.4.2 list prints lower-case $\rho_{\Delta inv}$ and $\eta_{t}^{\Delta inv}$. Tables 2, 3 and 5, Figure 3 and eq. (3) print $\rho_{\Delta INV}$ and $\eta^{\Delta INV}$.
4. **Employment indexation/Calvo subscript.** Tables 2 and 5 and Figure 3 write $\chi_{E}$ and $\xi_{E}$. Eq. (95) (PDF p.42, chunk T05) writes $\chi_{EM}$ and $\xi_{EM}$. Appendix D eq. (301) (PDF p.129, checked on the image) writes $\chi_{E}$ and $\xi_{E}$, matching the tables. They are the same parameters under two labels.
5. **World LR rate innovation.** It is $\eta_{t}^{*,fwd}$ in 4.4.2 and Table 4, but eq. (90) (PDF p.39) prints $\varepsilon_{t}^{*,fwd}$.
6. **Lagged-output coefficient in world trade.** Table 4 writes $c_{wt,y-}$; eq. (91) (PDF p.39) writes $c_{wt,y\_lag}$.
7. **Export competitors' relative price.**
   - 4.4.2 (p.52) prints $p_{X,t}^{c,*} = P_{X,t}^{c,*}/P_{t}^{*}$.
   - Section 3.6 (p.40) prints $p_{X,t}^{c,*} \equiv P_{X,t}^{c,*}/P_{Y,t}^{*} = 1$, with $P_{Y,t}^{*}$ in the denominator.
   - Table 1 writes the steady-state value as $p_{X}^{C}$ (capital C, no star).
8. **Shocks listed with an AR coefficient but never estimated or reported.**
   - Domestic premium $\rho_{DRP}$: Section 3.1, PDF p.14, prints a fixed 0.99 unit-root-type process with intercept (1−0.99).
   - World trade $\rho_{WT^{*}}$: eq. (91) has no own-AR term for the shock.
   - Oil price $\rho_{OIL}$: eq. (93) is an AR(2) in the relative oil price with coefficients $c_{oil,-}$ and $c_{oil,\Delta}$.

   The inflation target, VAT and world LR rate have fixed 0.99 coefficients in eqs. (64), (60) and (90), which is consistent with only an innovation being listed. A calibrating agent must take these fixed values from Section 3 (chunks T01/T04), not from this chunk.
9. **Counting.** The text says "25 structural shocks" and the printed list has exactly 25 entries. Table 3 has 14 observation-type S.D. rows (output deflator, employment, two term premia, ten EX innovations), matching the "14 observation equation shocks".
10. **Minor typos in prose (no model impact).**
    - "goods.." (double period) on PDF p.54.
    - "the the high volatility" on PDF p.53.
    - "Metropolis-Hasting" (sic) on pp.55 and 64.
11. **Table 3 prior std printed as "0.00"** for nine excess-trend S.D. rows (Hours worked through World trade). The Hours worked prior mean is also "0.00". With an inverse-gamma prior these are presumably rounded small values, not zeros. The table prints "Inf" for the other inverse-gamma prior stds. All are kept exactly as printed; a calibrating agent will need the unrounded values from another source.
12. **Calibrated values not in Table 1.**
    - $\gamma_{u,2}=10{,}000$ and $\chi_{W,g_{z}}=\gamma_{IM^{C}}=\gamma_{IM^{I}}=\gamma_{IM^{G}}=\gamma_{IM^{X}}=0$ appear only in the 4.4.1 text.
    - The long-run growth rates (hours 0.7%; $g_{\Delta Y^{*}}$ 2.2%, $g_{\Delta WT^{*}}$ 5.3%, $g_{\Delta C}$ 1.7%, $g_{\Delta I}$ 1.7%, $g_{\Delta X}$ 4.9%, $g_{\Delta IM}$ 2.9%, implied $g_{\Delta G}$ −1%, all annual) also appear only in the text. They are captured in T06.md, not in table1_calibrated.csv.
13. **Footnote 22.** $(1-\alpha) = 0.67$ while Table 1 gives $\alpha = 0.33$ (consistent). The text says the wage-bill share is 60%.
