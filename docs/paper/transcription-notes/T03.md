# T03 notes — 3.2.3 Domestic final goods firms, 3.2.4 Exporters, 3.2.5 Foreign retail firms (PDF pp. 24–30)

## Method
Every display and inline expression was read from the 300-DPI half-page images and zoomed at 600 DPI. As a cross-check, each glyph was decoded from the PDF's own font data: font family (cmmi/cmr/cmsy/cmex), glyph code, baseline and size, read with pdfminer. This settled ε vs ϵ (all `varepsilon`), φ vs ϕ (all `varphi`), ν vs v (all `nu`), the dagger and prime glyphs, sub/superscript positions, and the stacking of `c,*`. It also showed that the bars over Π are drawn as rules and the tildes over P are wide-tilde glyphs. None of the glyph readings is uncertain, so there are no ⚠ UNCERTAIN items.

## Suspected typos in the paper (all transcribed exactly as printed)

1. **(35), outer exponent, PDF p.24.** Printed as μ_C over μ_{C−1}: the "C−1" sits entirely on the subscript baseline (μ base 596.29; C, −, 1 all at 598.31). The economically expected exponent is μ_C/(μ_C − 1). Transcribed as `\frac{\mu_{C}}{\mu_{C-1}}`.
2. **(48), PDF p.28.** Inside a sum over k, the discount factor is printed Λ_{t,t−1} and the profit term is dated t: S_t P_{X,f^X,t} X_{f^X,t} − MC_t^X X_{f^X,t}. The domestic analogue (27) uses Λ_{t,t+k} and t+k dating throughout.
3. **(50), PDF p.28.** The discount factor is printed Λ_{t,t+1} instead of Λ_{t,t+k}, as in (28). The exchange-rate term is printed S_t^{−1}, dated t; the import analogue (33) divides by S_{t+k}.
4. **Definition of Π†_{X,t,t+k} after (50), PDF p.28 (blocks 50.i1, 50.i2):**
   - χ_X appears twice: Π_{X,t+s−1} carries the superscript χ_X inside the parentheses, and the parenthesis carries a second exponent χ_X, i.e. (Π^{χ_X}_{X,t+s−1})^{χ_X}. The analogues after (28) and (33) have a single exponent χ.
   - The foreign objective is written Π̄*_{X,t+s}, with subscript X. In (47) it is Π̄*_t.
   - The boundary case is printed "Π†_{X,t,t+k} ≡ 1 for k = 1". This contradicts the k ≥ 1 case; the analogues use k = 0.
5. **(51), PDF p.29.** The foreign objective appears as Π̄*_{X,t}, with subscript X. Equation (47) has Π̄*_t without it.
6. **Footnote 14, PDF p.24.** It says "Section 3.2.3 below elaborates on … export goods", but the footnote is attached to 3.2.3 itself. The export material is in 3.2.4–3.2.5. This is prose only and has no math block.

## Other observations (not necessarily errors)
- **Exporter index letter case.** The index is printed in lowercase as f^x in the text of 3.2.4 (p.21: "f^x ∈ [0,1]", "X_{f^x,t}", "exporter f^x"; p.22: "an exporter f^x"). Elsewhere it is uppercase f^X: (46)–(54), "firm f^X" on p.22, and "f^X ∈ [0,1]" on p.23. Both are kept as printed; the symbol table lists both.
- **Γ† definitions (44.i1 and (57)).** The derivative term is multiplied by the level IM_t^C or X_t, not by the ratio IM_t^C/Q_t^C or X_t/WT_t^*. The paper does not say what Γ′ is differentiated with respect to. 44.i1 uses "=", while (57) uses "≡", both as printed.
- **Parentheses in (47).** The exponent (1−χ_X) is printed in parentheses. In 50.i1 and (51) the exponent 1−χ_X has none. Both kept as printed.
- **Punctuation and bracket sizes.** (49) has a space before its final period, and (44), (45), (55) have their closing punctuation set apart; all trailing punctuation was dropped per the conventions. Inner parentheses are one size larger in (36), (37), (52), (54) than in (41), (42). Size and spacing are not transcribed.
- **Typesetting of accents.** The bar over Π is drawn as a rule (overline), as it also is in (33)–(34) on p.24. The tilde over P is a wide-tilde glyph (`\widetilde`). Both are transcribed as `\bar{\Pi}` and `\tilde{P}`, following the conventions for single-letter accents.
- **ε^X.** The shock ε_t^X in (55)–(57) is not separately defined in this chunk. By analogy with ε_t^IM in (38), it is presumably a shock to export adjustment costs. The symbol table states that it is not defined here.
- **Generalization paragraph, p.21.** Equations (35)–(45) apply to sectors I, G and X with C replaced, except that the exported-final-good price is P_{DX,t}. Only this notation is captured, as inline block 45.i1; no equations are implied or added.
