## Suspected typos in the paper (transcribed as printed; not "fixed")
1. **(214), $\hat{g}$ terms.** Printed: `+ (β ω (1−ω) ĝ_{z,t} − 1) + ω ĝ_{z,t−1}`. A
   log-linearization of the main-text investment FOC (9)–(11) gives the coefficient
   $(\beta\omega_{\Gamma_I}(1-\omega_{\Gamma_I}) - 1)$ multiplying $\hat{g}_{z,t}$ (i.e. the
   "−1" belongs inside the coefficient, not added as a constant), and $-\omega_{\Gamma_I}
   \hat{g}_{z,t-1}$ (negative sign). The leads and the investment terms of (214) match that
   derivation. Checked numerically (finite-difference log-linearization of (11) with (10),
   script in the (working image, not included) `ana/inv_check.py`): all printed investment and lead coefficients
   reproduce to 6 decimals; the $\hat g_{z,t}$ coefficient is $(\beta\omega(1-\omega)-1)/D$ and
   the $\hat g_{z,t-1}$ coefficient is $-\omega/D$.
2. **(214), exponent.** Printed $\gamma_{I} g_{z}^{2(1-\omega_{\Gamma_{I}})}$. With the main-text
   adjustment cost (10), $\Gamma_I = \frac{\gamma_I}{2}(\tilde I - g_z^{1+\omega_{\Gamma_I}})^2$,
   the steady-state second derivative is $\gamma_I$ and the slope term would involve
   $g_{z}^{2(1+\omega_{\Gamma_{I}})}$. Printed sign inside the exponent confirmed as "−" by zoom.
   The same numerical check gives the coefficient on $\hat Q_t$ as
   $1/(D\gamma_I g_z^{2(1+\omega)})$ (0.220366 vs 0.221580 for the printed exponent, at
   $\beta=0.995$, $\omega=0.55$, $\gamma_I=3$, $g_z=1.0025$).
3. **(217), coefficient of the expected inflation differential.** Log-linearizing (4), (6),
   (7) gives $(1-\gamma_S)$ on the whole expected nominal depreciation
   $E_t(\hat s_{t+1}-\hat s_t+\hat\pi_{Y,t+1}-\hat\pi^{*}_{Y,t+1})$; the paper prints
   coefficient 1 on $E_{t}(\hat{\pi}_{Y,t+1}-\hat{\pi}^{*}_{Y,t+1})$. Also the premium
   coefficient is printed $\gamma_{B}$ (main text: $\gamma_{B^{*}}$), and the lower-case
   $\hat{\pi}^{*}_{y,t}$ — possibly the same variable as $\hat{\pi}^{*}_{Y,t}$.
4. **(225), sign in the denominators.** Printed $1-\nu^{f}(R-1)$ twice. The main text defines
   $R_{t}^{F}=1+\nu^{F}(R_{t}-1)$ (PDF p.21), whose log-linearization has denominator
   $1+\nu^{F}(R-1)$. Also $\hat{\epsilon}^{F}_{t}$ and $\hat{\nu}_{f,t}$ are not defined in the
   paper's text (no AR process for them found in D.6–D.11 text layer).
5. **(233), $\nu_{E}$.** The home-bias coefficient is printed $\frac{\nu_E}{1-\nu_E}$; the
   analogous (237), (241), (245) use the sector's own weight, so this is presumably $\nu_{C}$.
6. **(246), $\hat{\epsilon}^{X}_{t}$** where (234), (238), (242) have $\hat{\epsilon}^{IM}_{t}$.
   In the main text $\varepsilon^{X}_{t}$ is the shock in the exporters' world-trade-share
   adjustment cost (eq. (56), PDF p.30), a different mechanism from the import-share cost of
   final-export producers; so $\hat{\epsilon}^{X}_{t}$ here is probably a typo for
   $\hat{\epsilon}^{IM}_{t}$. Flagged only (both shocks are switched off in estimation, PDF p.52).
7. **(248), duplicated term.** The second term on the first line is printed
   $\frac{\chi_X}{1+\beta\chi_X}[E_t(\hat{\bar\pi}^*_{t+1})-\hat{\bar\pi}^*_t]$, identical in
   content to the first term of the second line (which has coefficient
   $\frac{\beta\chi_X}{1+\beta\chi_X}$). By analogy with (226)/(229) and the export indexation
   scheme (47), the first-line term should be the lagged-inflation term
   $\frac{\chi_X}{1+\beta\chi_X}(\hat\pi_{X,t-1}-\hat{\bar\pi}^*_t)$. As printed, (248) has no
   lagged export inflation.
8. **(212), tax coefficient.** Printed $-\frac{1}{1+0.78\,\tau^{c}}\hat{\tau}^{C}_{t}$, i.e. the
   numerical value 0.78 (= $w_{\tau^C}$, Table 1) instead of the symbol, and lower-case
   $\tau^{c}$. Log-linearizing (5), $\Lambda \propto 1/(1+w_{\tau^C}\tau^{C}_t)$, with
   $\hat{\tau}^{C}$ an absolute deviation (as the tax deviations are used in (213), (219),
   (223) and in observation eq. (314)) gives $-\frac{0.78}{1+0.78\,\tau^{c}}$; the printed
   numerator 1 matches only if $\hat{\tau}^{C}$ is the deviation of $w_{\tau^C}\tau^{C}$.
   Possible typo; flagged only. The habit/consumption/productivity terms of (212) agree with (5).
