## Other observations

- **Equation (32) appears only once.** `check_chunk.py` lists 32 in KNOWN_DUPLICATES ("the paper prints (32) twice (verify!)"). On T02's pages, (32) is printed exactly once (p.17 / pdf p.23). A `pdftotext` search of the whole PDF also finds the label "(32)" only once, plus the in-text reference "maximize (32)" on the same page. So T02 has one (32) block. No other duplicate numbers occur in (17)-(34).
- **Accent widths.** In this chunk the bar over $\Pi$ is printed full-width and the tilde over $P$ is printed wide. On p.12 (eq. (16), T01's range) the same objects use narrow accents. I treated this as typography and wrote `\bar{\Pi}` and `\tilde{P}`, following CONVENTIONS 4.5 for a single letter, as chunk T05 also does.
- **The bar has two meanings here.** $\bar{\Pi}_{t}$ is the gross time-varying inflation objective (26), while $\bar{\Pi}_{Y}^{*}$ is gross steady-state foreign inflation (30).
- In (30) the oil price enters lagged ($P_{OIL,t-1}^{*}$), although the text defines $P_{OIL,t}^{*}$. This is intended: footnote 12 says the oil-price effect is delayed. It is not a typo.
- Integral limits in (19), (22) and 21.i1 are printed above and below the integral sign. I wrote `\int_{0}^{1}`, which is a typographic difference only.
- Figure 1 prints the foreign asterisks after the time subscript ("WT_t*", "X_t*"). I wrote them as superscripts in the figure description.
- $\nu^{F}$ was compared at 600 DPI with Computer Modern renderings of `\nu` and `v`. It is Greek nu.
- **Referenced but not defined in T02:**
  - From Section 3.1: $R_{K,t}$, $R_{t}$, $\Lambda_{h,t}$, $P_{C,t}$, $\beta$.
  - From 3.2.3 onward (T03): demand equations (39) and (40) and price index (41).
  - No explicit formula is given for $\Lambda_{t,t+k}^{*}$; the paper only calls it the foreign counterpart.
  - No stochastic process for $\varepsilon_{t}$ is given in this chunk.
  - $\varphi_{t}^{H}$ and $\varphi_{t}^{*}$ are named as markups. Footnote 11 says all markups follow the AR(1) form of (20).
