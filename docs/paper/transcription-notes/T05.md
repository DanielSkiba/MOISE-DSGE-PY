# T05 issues log

Scope: Section 4 opening and 4.1–4.3 (PDF pp. 40–47, printed 34–41), then Appendix A and Appendix B (PDF pp. 93–100, printed 87–94; B.4 ends three lines into PDF p.101, which is described in prose only). Numbered equations: (95)–(109), each transcribed once.

## Suspected typos / inconsistencies in the paper (all transcribed as printed)

1. **Eq. (106), p.93 / PDF p.99: "$\hat{w}_{t} + \hat{w}_{t-1}$"**. Zoomed at 600 DPI (working image, not included) the sign is clearly "+". Every other observation equation in Appendix B, (99)–(105) and (107), uses the change $\hat{x}_{t} - \hat{x}_{t-1}$. The text also says (106) links wage growth to the real-wage deviation, so "$-$" is almost certainly intended. Implementers should probably use $\hat{w}_{t} - \hat{w}_{t-1}$ and check this against the paper's Appendix D.10 observation equations (chunk T13).
2. **p.94 / PDF p.100, the text after (107)**: the subscript is printed "$OIL.t$", with a period, in both $\hat{p}_{OIL.t}^{*}$ and $p_{OIL.t}^{*}$. Everywhere else it is "$OIL,t$". This is probably a typo for a comma. Transcribed as printed in 107.i3; the inline $\hat{p}_{OIL.t}^{*}$ is only mentioned in the Note, since it is just the hatted name of the variable defined there. Zoom: (working image, not included) and (working image, not included)
3. **Log-difference definition, printed twice in different forms.** p.34 (PDF p.40) prints $\Delta X_{t} \equiv \log\left(\frac{X_{t}}{X_{t-1}}\right)$, upper case with a stacked fraction (block 94.i2). p.87 (PDF p.93) prints $\Delta X_{t} \equiv \log\left(x_{t}/x_{t-1}\right)$, with lower-case $x$ inside the log and a slash (block 97.i101). Both are transcribed as printed. The lower-case $x$ on p.87 is probably notational sloppiness: it does not mean the stationarized variable.
4. **Current account definition, printed twice with different relation signs:** "$=$" on p.35 (94.i4) and "$\equiv$" on p.90 (97.i104).
5. **Generic observation equation (95.u1), p.39:** it has $J^{g} \cdot \left(\hat{g}_{z,t} + g_{z}\right)$ with no "$-1$". The explicit equations (101) and (104) have $\hat{g}_{z,t} + \left(g_{z}-1\right)$, and (106) has $\left(\hat{g}_{z,t} + g_{z} - 1\right)$. The generic form is probably missing the "$-1$" ($g_{z}$ is a gross rate). Transcribed as printed.
6. **Footnote 54 (98.i1/98.i2):** it defines $y_{t} = \log(Y_{t}/z_{t})$ and then says "$y$ is the steady state value of $Y_{t}/z_{t}$". For $\hat{y}_{t} = y_{t} - y$ to be a log deviation, $y$ must be the steady state of the log ratio. This is a minor wording slip; transcribed as printed.
7. **Eq. (104):** $j^{H}$ is printed without its argument "$(J)$", whereas (102) and the indicator definition write $j^{H}\left(J\right)$. Kept without the argument, as printed.
8. **Eq. (107):** the foreign-inflation term is the observed-data symbol $\Delta P_{Y,t}^{*}$, not a model variable (compare (105), which uses $\hat{\pi}_{Y,t}^{*} + \bar{\Pi}^{*} - 1$). Kept as printed; implementers must decide whether it means the observed G4 CPI growth or model foreign inflation.
9. **Figure 2 (p.38 / PDF p.44):** the PDF text layer has a second, hidden note inside the figure ("Note: Trends determind by stochastic growth (gz) and additive components disscussed in section 3.1. Interest rates are annualized"). It is not visible in the rendered image, so it is not transcribed. The visible note under the figure refers to section 4.2.
10. **Nominal exchange rate weights:** 49% + 32% + 13% + 6% = 100%. They are given twice (pp. 89 and 90) and agree.

## Glyph decisions (no remaining uncertainty after zooming)

- **$\hat{\bar{\pi}}_{t}$ in (105), (106), and $\hat{\bar{\pi}}_{t}^{*}$ in (109) and the B.4 text:** a wide hat printed over a bar over $\pi$. Written `\hat{\bar{\pi}}`, as in EXAMPLE.md for the same variable. Zoom: (working image, not included) (working image, not included)
- **Hats on single letters:**
  - The hat on $N$ in (95) is printed wide (`\widehat`-style); in (99) it is narrow. Both are written `\hat{N}` (single-letter rule).
  - The hat on $g_{z,t}$ in the p.39 display (95.u1) and the following text is printed wide; it is written `\hat{g}_{z,t}`, matching (101), (104) and (106), where it is narrow.
  - $\widehat{EM}$ and $\widehat{rr}$ use `\widehat` (multi-letter).
- **$\hat{\jmath}_{t}$ in (104) and the text above it:** a dotless j with a hat (the text layer shows "|^"). Zoom: (working image, not included)
- **Shocks:** $\varepsilon_{t}^{EM}$ in (95) and $\varepsilon_{t}^{fwd,OB}$, $\varepsilon_{t}^{*,fwd,OB}$ in (108)–(109) are the reversed-3 form (`\varepsilon`). The innovations $\eta_{GR,t}^{N}$, $\eta_{EX,t}^{J}$ are Greek eta.
- **Superscripts:** $\chi_{EM}$ and $\xi_{EM}$ in (95) are chi and xi (working image, not included) $\rho$ in (98) and (102) is the ordinary rho. $\bar{\Pi}$ and $\bar{\Pi}^{*}$ are capital Pi with a bar. In (107), $EX_{t}^{P_{OIL}^{*}}$ is a superscript $P$ that itself carries the subscript $OIL$ and superscript $*$.
- **Indicator function display (102.u1):** "if" is printed in math italic, so it is kept as `if` in an `array`, with no `\text`. The display ends with a lone period, which is dropped.
- **Uppercase $J$ in $J^{g}$, $J^{N}$ (p.39):** these are selection indicators, unrelated to the component index $J$ in B.2. Both use the same glyph in print.

## Other notes

- **Ids at chunk boundaries:** the convention `<last eq>.i<k>` gives Part 1's first inline blocks the prefix 94. The chunk T04 may also have 94.i blocks, for example the definition on p.40 just above the "4 Estimation" heading ($p_{X,t}^{c,*} \equiv P_{X,t}^{c,*}/P_{Y,t}^{*} = 1$, which is T04's). T04.md, written meanwhile, does use 94.i1 for that definition. To avoid collisions, T05's Part 1 inline ids start at **94.i2**: 94.i2–94.i5. For the same reason Appendix A's ids start at **97.i101**: 97.i101–97.i104. They follow 97.i1 (Section 4.3) and stay below Appendix B's 98.*. The caller may renumber if the convention requires gapless numbering.
- **Text outside the boundaries:** the definition above the "4 Estimation" heading on p.40 is not transcribed (it belongs to T04). The three lines on p.101 that end B.4 are summarized in prose only; they contain no new math beyond symbols already defined.
- **Appendix A has no numbered table.** It is a bulleted list, converted to a Markdown table and to `data/appendixA_data.csv` (24 rows).

The number is correct. It is missed only because the tool's regex rejects a number followed by a period ("2006.").
