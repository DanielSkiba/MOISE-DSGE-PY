## Notation remarks for implementers (not errors)

- **Π vs π̄.** The opening text names the inflation target π̄, a lower-case pi with a bar. The steady-state equations (111), (112) and (138) use capital Π (and Π*) for steady-state gross inflation. The chunk never states Π = π̄ explicitly; it only says every steady-state inflation rate is consistent with the target. 112.i1 adds the assumption Π* = Π.
- **Capital timing.** Normalized capital is defined as k_t ≡ K_{t+1}/z_t (113.i1), with end-of-period capital over current productivity. That is why (118) carries the factor (g_z)^{−α}.
- **World-trade scaling.** Scaled world trade is divided by *foreign* productivity, wt*_t = WT*_t/z*_t (137.i1), not by z_t. Relative technology is z̃_t = z*_t/z_t (137.i2).
- **CPI numeraire.** Because P_C is the numeraire, the consumption-sector price index (132) is written with 1 on the left. The consumption-sector demands (124) and (128) use p_H and p_IM without dividing by a sector price.
- **(110.i1) brackets.** λ_t = (Λ_t · z_t) is printed with parentheses and a centred dot, and is transcribed that way.
- **(138) layout.** The paper prints (138) eqnarray-style with a wide "="; it is transcribed with `aligned`, keeping the paper's line break.
