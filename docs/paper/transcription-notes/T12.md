# T12 issues log — D.6–D.9 (eqs 251–290, PDF pp.123–128, printed pp.117–122)

All 40 numbered equations (251)–(290) are transcribed, one block each. There are no unnumbered displays and no defining inline math in this range: the pages hold only short labels followed by numbered equations.

No glyph remained uncertain after zooming to 600–3000 DPI. Where accent placement was ambiguous (narrow vs wide hats on two-letter names), I rendered candidate LaTeX at the same size and compared connected-component positions with the original. The decisions are below.

## Accent / notation decisions (verified by measurement, not typos)
- (259) `\hat{rr}_{t}^{fw}`: a narrow hat centred over both letters "rr" (centred 0.497 of the pair width). It is not `r\hat{r}` and not a wide hat.
- (262) LHS `\widehat{rr}_{t}^{fw}` has a wide hat, 5.62 pt. The summands `\hat{ri}_{t+20}` and `\hat{ri}_{t+39}` have narrow hats centred over "ri".
- (261) LHS `\widehat{ri}_{t}`, wide hat.
- (283) LHS `\hat{im}_{t}`: a narrow hat centred over "im", whereas the right-hand side uses `\widehat{im}`.
- (276) and (280) `\hat{\Delta inv}_{t}`: a narrow hat centred over the whole "Δinv" group (centre at 0.501 of the group width, at the height of a hat over Δ). It is not `\Delta\hat{i}nv` (0.435) and not `\Delta\hat{in}v` (0.572). In (284) the term is `\Delta\widehat{inv}_{t}` with a wide hat over "inv" only.
- (280) `p_{\hat{I},t}`: a full-size hat at accent height centred over the subscript I, rendered position matches. A scan of every hat over p in the chunk found only one other irregular case: (284) line 1, where `\hat{p}_{I,t}` has its hat shifted 2.5 pt right instead of the normal 1.05 pt. Both are transcribed as printed (`p_{\hat{I},t}` and `\hat{p}_{I,t}` respectively) and noted.
- (260) `\eta_{t}^{\overline{\Pi}}`: the bar spans the full width of Π (6.4 pt), so `\overline`.
- (264), (286), (287), (289): the shock or technology symbol is the lunate `\epsilon` (with a middle bar), unlike the main text's `\varepsilon`.
- (271) `\hat{\tilde{z}}` is a hat over a tilde; `\hat{\nu}^{*}` is Greek nu.
- (276), (280), (284) investment `\hat{\imath}_{t}`: a dotless i with a hat.

## Suspected typos / inconsistencies in the paper (transcribed as printed)
1. (254) The LHS superscript is lowercase `k` (`\hat{\tau}_{t}^{k}`), while the lag, `\rho_{\tau^{K}}` and `\eta^{\tau^{K}}` use uppercase K.
2. (259) The bracket has `\hat{\bar{\pi}}_{t}` first, but the gap is `(\hat{\pi}_{t}^{CB} - \bar{\pi}_{t})` with no hat on the target. `y_{t}^{GAP}` has no hat (also in 264). The superscript is `fw`. Main-text (62) has `\widehat{rr}^{fwd}`, hat-bar in both places, and `\hat{y}^{GAP}`.
3. (261) `\hat{\pi}_{C,t+1}` has no `E_{t}`. Main text p.26 has `E_{t}`.
4. (262) The sum runs t+20…t+39. Main-text (63) runs t+21…t+40.
5. (265) Has the factor 0.25. Main-text (65) has no 0.25 and puts all terms inside `E_{t}[...]`.
6. (273) `\hat{s}_{t} = \hat{c}_{t} + \hat{p}_{Y,t}`: the RHS `\hat{c}` is also the symbol for consumption (275). The analogous export terms in (282) and (284) also print `\hat{c}_{t}`, where (280) prints `\hat{s}_{t}` (verified at 3000 DPI). Ambiguous notation, or an error.
7. (280) The line-2 denominator is `p_{Y} g`, probably meant to be `p_{Y} y`. The government price is `\hat{p}_{g,t}` with a lowercase g; (284) has `\hat{p}_{G,t}`. The line-1 denominators mix `p_{y} y`, `P_{y} y` and `P_{Y}`.
8. (281) `\hat{p}_{y,t}` has a lowercase y, where `\hat{p}_{Y,t}` is used elsewhere.
9. (282) The denominators are `P_{Y} Y`, with capital P and capital Y, where lowercase steady-state symbols are used elsewhere.
10. (284) The consumption tax is written `\tau_{c}` and `\hat{\tau}_{t}^{c}` with lowercase c, versus `\hat{\tau}^{C}` in (252). There are capital `P_{IM}` numerators in two terms. The line-5 first denominator is ordered `y p_{Y}^{M}`. Line 3 has lowercase `p_{x} p_{y}`.
11. (285) `\hat{h}_{s,t}` has s as a subscript; (274) has `\hat{h}_{t}^{s}` (superscript).
12. (287) The foreign target `\bar{\pi}_{t}^{*}` has no hat; main-text (94) has a hat-bar. The inflation subscripts are lowercase `y`. Note also that (287) puts `0.2 E_{t}(...)` inside the bracket, where the main text writes `(...)/5`.
13. (288) The LHS is `\hat{\pi}_{y,t}^{*}` (lowercase) while the RHS uses `\hat{\pi}_{Y}^{*}`. The last term is `+\hat{\pi}_{Y,t}^{*}`, which is the LHS variable itself, where main-text (92) has the shock `\varepsilon_{t}^{\Pi^{*}}`. This is very likely a typo for the shock. The oil price has no star here (main text: `\hat{p}^{*}_{OIL}`).
14. (289) The persistence coefficient is printed with a star, `c_{wt,-}^{*}`. The shock subscript is `IM_{t}^{*}` with t inside. The coefficient `c_{wt,y-}` corresponds to `c_{wt,y\_lag}` in main-text (91).
15. (290) The LHS subscript is "OILl", with a stray lowercase l. The lagged-change term has a minus, `-c_{OIL,\Delta}`, whereas main-text (93) has a plus, `+c_{oil,\Delta}`. Implementers should check which sign is used in the estimated model.
16. (286) The real-rate gap is written `\widehat{ri}^{*}_{t} - \widehat{fw}_{ri^{*},t}`. Main-text (89) writes `\hat{r}^{*}_{t} - E_{t}[\hat{\pi}^{*}_{Y,t+1}] - \widehat{rr}^{*,fwd}_{t}` (same concept, different notation; `\widehat{ri}^{*}` is not defined in the appendix).
17. (272) The time index sits inside the subscript (`s_{B_{t+1}^{*}}`), and no hats are printed.
